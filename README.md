# Food Loop

Helping Louisiana college students plan and manage their food budgets.

**Live site:** [bayou-basket-scout.lovable.app](https://bayou-basket-scout.lovable.app/)
## What it does

Food Loop helps Louisiana college students plan meals on a budget. It has one shared budget across:

- **Cook with groceries:** meal ideas that fit your equipment, pantry, and budget
- **Ready to eat:** pickup from local restaurants, corner stores, mini-marts, and gas-station kitchens
- **My Plan:** a saved meal plan and shopping list
- **Food assistance:** SNAP contacts and local food resources in Louisiana

## Why the project is useful?

Many students have limited kitchen access, limited transportation, and tight budgets. Food Loop plans around those limits. It also notes that a low price doesn't always mean a healthy meal.
